Why study in science degree?

A science degree provides you with:

  • Critical thinking and problem-solving skills
  • Strong foundation for various career paths
  • Opportunities for research and innovation
  • Understanding of the natural world and technology
  • Preparation for advanced studies in specialized fields

Our science programs are designed to foster curiosity and develop analytical skills that are valuable in any profession.

What you can study?

We offer a wide range of programs:

  • Intermediate Programs: Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, Computer Science, Statistics, Economics
  • Bachelor Programs: BS Physics with Data Science and BS Computer Science
  • Skills: Freelancing, Graphic Designing, and Digital Marketing

Each program is carefully designed with modern curricula and taught by experienced faculty members.

Admission Information

Admission Criteria

  • Must have passed SSC (Matriculation) or equivalent
  • Minimum required marks: 65% (varies by program)
  • Offered Groups:
    • Pre-Medical
    • Pre-Engineering
    • ICS (Computer Science)
    • General Science
  • Admissions are based on merit

Documents Required

  • Original SSC result card + photocopies
  • Character certificate
  • Form-B or CNIC
  • Passport-size photos (2 copies)
  • Migration certificate (if from another board)
  • Affidavit Document
  • Father's Occupation Documents

Admission Criteria

  • Must have passed HSSC/Intermediate (FSc) or equivalent
  • Minimum marks: 50%
  • For BS Computer Science: FSc Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical, or ICS with Mathematics — minimum 50%
  • For BS Physics with Data Science: FSc Pre-Medical or Pre-Engineering with Physics & Mathematics — minimum 45%
  • Offered BS Programs:
    • BS Computer Science
    • BS Physics with Data Science (Special focus: AI, ML & Quantum Computing)
  • Duration: 4 years (8 semesters)
  • Merit-based admission — no entry test unless announced
  • Separate applications for each BS program
  • Interview or department test may be conducted depending on department policy
