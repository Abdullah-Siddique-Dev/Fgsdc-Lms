💻 BS COMPUTER SCIENCE

BS Computer Science is a 4-year degree programme with a special focus on Software Engineering (SE), Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Cyber Security, and Cloud Computing. The programme builds strong foundations in computing and prepares students for careers in the most in-demand tech fields.

Programme Overview

  • Programming Fundamentals & Data Structures
  • Software Engineering (SE)
  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • Cyber Security
  • Cloud Computing
  • Database Systems & Big Data
  • Computer Networks
  • Web & Mobile Application Development

Duration

4-year programme with a total of 8 semesters. Each year will have 2 semesters — Spring and Fall semesters.

Admission Criteria & Eligibility

  • FSc Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical, or ICS with Mathematics from a recognised Pakistani board
  • Minimum 50% marks in FSc
  • Mathematics is a compulsory subject at intermediate level
  • Selection is merit-based

Note: The College is affiliated with National University of Pakistan for BS Programmes. All BS degrees are awarded by the National University of Pakistan.
