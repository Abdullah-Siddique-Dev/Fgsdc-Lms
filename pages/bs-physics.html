⚛️ BS PROGRAMME

BS Physics with Data Science is a 4-year degree programme with a special focus on Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Quantum Computing. The programme equips students with a strong foundation in core physics alongside cutting-edge computational and data science skills, preparing them for careers in modern technology, research, and innovation.

Programme Overview

  • Classical Mechanics and Electromagnetism
  • Thermodynamics and Statistical Physics
  • Principles of Waves and Optics
  • Quantum Mechanics & Quantum Computing
  • Modern Physics and Laboratory Work
  • Mathematical Methods in Physics
  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • Data Science and Scientific Computing

Duration

4-year programme with a total of 8 semesters. Each year will have 2 semesters — Spring and Fall semesters.

Admission Criteria & Eligibility

  • Must hold an FSc (HSSC) certificate — Pre-Medical or Pre-Engineering (Mathematics & Physics must be included)
  • Minimum 45% marks in FSc
  • Physics and Mathematics are compulsory subjects at intermediate level
  • Selection is merit-based

Note: The College is affiliated with NUP (National University of Pakistan) for BS Programmes. All BS degrees are awarded by the National University of Pakistan.
